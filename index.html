📦 Circular No. 243/2025 — Partial Dispensing of Individually Packaged Medications
📅 04 Dec 2025 ⭐ Critical All Pharmacies
  • Effective 1 Jan 2026: dispense exactly the prescribed quantity = Dose × Frequency × Duration
  • Rounding up to nearest full strip is permitted — rounding down is NOT allowed
  • Submit dispensed quantity as a fraction of the commercial pack in the claim
  • Applies to all Abu Dhabi pharmacies — outpatient and inpatient
❌ Exempt from Partial Dispensing:
Vaccines & Biologics
Moisture/Light/Temperature-sensitive
Calendar packaging (contraceptives)
Solutions & Syrups
Topical Creams & Gels
Effervescent formulations

🔄 PBM Cancellation Process — Full Pharmacy Protocol
📅 12 Aug 2026 🆕 New Pharmacies Ref: PND/SA/AC/26/UAE-39
🚨
Golden Rule: Once "Dispense" is selected OR a claim is submitted (via Eclaim, Riayati, or billed in POS) — cancellation requests must NOT be raised. Manual cancellations require formal notification and a complex, time-consuming process. Providers with excessive cancellations will be subject to review and remedial measures.
✅ Complete Checklist — BEFORE Initiating Any PBM Transaction:
#CheckDetails & Requirements
1 🪪 Patient Eligibility Verify eligibility and ensure dispensing is within the card validity/expiry date.
⭐ Exception: THIQA Category 1 (C1) patients may be dispensed the full prescribed duration regardless of card expiry — follow the PBM approval letter instead.
2 🤝 Patient Confirmation Confirm all prescribed medications are required and the patient agrees to receive ALL items.
Ensure the patient is aware of the applicable copayment/cost share and obtain confirmation before dispensing.
3 📦 Stock Availability Confirm medication is available in stock before initiating any PBM transaction.
4 🚚 Home Delivery Before dispatch, confirm that the patient requires and will receive all prescribed medications. Do not dispatch without this confirmation.
5 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Details Verify the doctor code is correct and the doctor's specialty is appropriate for the patient's age, gender, and diagnosis. Diagnosis must be within the doctor's scope of practice.
6 🔢 Diagnosis Code Ensure the correct ICD-10 diagnosis code is selected — it must accurately correspond to the diagnosis on the prescription and clinically support the medication being prescribed.
7 🩺 Clinical Appropriateness Confirm the medication and dose are clinically justified and appropriate for the patient's age, gender, and diagnosis. Check against relevant circulars and guidelines.
8 💊 Medication Accuracy Verify: correct medication name · correct concentration · correct dosage form · correct quantity.
Quantity = Dose × Frequency × Duration (as prescribed by the doctor).
9 📋 Circulars Compliance Ensure compliance with all previously shared circulars, guidelines, and insurance requirements (duration limits, prior auth rules, specialty restrictions, etc.).
⚙️ System Requirements:
  • Pharmacy PBM systems must support automatic cancellation for approved requests where medications have NOT yet been dispensed to the patient
  • Manual cancellations are strongly discouraged and should only occur for un-dispensed medications
  • Submit PBM request only after completing ALL checks above
  • Action "Dispense" or submit claim only after medications are physically handed to the patient
⚠️
Strict compliance required to avoid rejected claims, audit findings, and unnecessary operational issues. Non-compliance will result in review and appropriate remedial measures by Daman.

⚡ Universal Rules for ALL Specialty Drugs

  • Prior Authorization (PA) is mandatory for all drugs in this section — dispensing without PA = claim rejection
  • ICD-10 and drug codes must be coded to the highest level of specificity
  • All specialty drugs are NOT covered for Visitor's Plan unless explicitly stated otherwise
  • Prescriptions must be from eligible specialist — GP prescriptions are rejected
  • Medical reports must be signed and stamped by treating physician
  • Questionnaire submission is required for most drugs — check each guideline

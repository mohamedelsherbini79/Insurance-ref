How this works Each tool keeps its own data — they don't share patient records, notes, or shortages with each other. This page is just a shared front door: keep all 5 files (this one + the 4 tools) together in the same folder, whether on your computer or uploaded to your GitHub Pages site, and the links above will work. Each tool still has its own employee list, so add your name once per tool the first time you use it.
